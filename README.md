# 🪿 Gooose Desktop

### Your Desktop. Their Playground.

**A mischievous goose has moved into your Mac. And it's not here to behave.**

Gooose is a playful, open-source desktop pet for macOS.  
It steals your cursor, drags memes across your screen, leaves messages, and brings a little chaos to your desktop.

## More than just a desktop pet

- Multiple geese with independent interactions
- Customizable personalities
- Custom sounds and memes
- Interactive desktop pranks
- Native macOS experience

**And yes, they really do steal your mouse.**
